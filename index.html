Digital ordering: Problems & solutions

Encountering difficulties when using self-service kiosks or mobile ordering applications? Find practical solutions to common digital ordering problems below.

Payment was declined.

What to do:

  • Check your available balance.
  • Verify that your payment details are correct.
  • Try another payment method accepted by the restaurant if the transaction remains unsuccessful.

QR payment is not scanning.

What to do:

  • Adjust your phone's screen brightness.
  • Make sure the QR code is clearly visible.
  • Position your phone properly in front of the scanner.
  • If scanning still fails, ask restaurant staff for assistance.

Card payment is unsuccessful.

What to do:

  • Check whether your card is valid and has not expired.
  • Verify that sufficient funds are available.
  • Ask restaurant staff to check the payment terminal if the problem continues.

Payment is still pending.

What to do:

  • Check the transaction status in your payment application.
  • Keep your transaction reference number.
  • Avoid repeating the payment until the previous transaction is confirmed.

The payment was deducted, but the order was not confirmed.

What to do:

  • Check your payment application or bank account to confirm whether the amount was deducted.
  • Save a screenshot of the transaction as proof of payment.
  • Inform the restaurant staff and provide your transaction reference number.
  • Avoid making another payment until the issue is resolved.

Kiosk screen is lagging.

What to do:

  • Wait for the current selection to respond before tapping again.
  • Avoid repeatedly pressing the same button.
  • Ask restaurant staff for assistance if the screen remains slow.

Touchscreen is not responding.

What to do:

  • Avoid repeatedly tapping the screen.
  • Wait briefly for the system to respond.
  • Notify restaurant staff if the touchscreen remains unresponsive.

Mobile application is running slowly.

What to do:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Close unnecessary applications.
  • Reopen the ordering application and check whether it is working properly.

Application suddenly crashes.

What to do:

  • Reopen the application.
  • Check your order history to see whether your order was submitted.
  • If the application continues to crash, check for available updates.

Menu is not loading.

What to do:

  • Check your internet connection if using a mobile application.
  • Refresh the menu page.
  • For kiosk problems, ask restaurant staff for assistance.

Wrong food item is selected.

What to do:

  • Return to the menu.
  • Select the correct food item.
  • Review your order summary before submitting.

Ordered item becomes unavailable.

What to do:

  • Check whether another available option is offered.
  • Ask restaurant staff about possible alternatives before proceeding.

Duplicate items appear in the cart.

What to do:

  • Review the items in your cart.
  • Remove the extra item before confirming your purchase.

Received food does not match the confirmed order.

What to do:

  • Check your receipt or order confirmation.
  • Present it to restaurant staff.
  • Identify the incorrect or missing food item and request assistance.

Wrong meal size is displayed.

What to do:

  • Review the selected meal size.
  • Return to the customization options if necessary.
  • Correct the selection before submitting the order.

No order confirmation appears.

What to do:

  • Check your order history or receipt, if available.
  • Check your payment transaction if you have already paid.
  • Ask restaurant staff to verify whether your order was received before placing another order.

Order is taking longer than expected.

What to do:

  • Keep your order number.
  • Present it to restaurant staff.
  • Ask for an update on your order's preparation status.

No order notification is received.

What to do:

  • Open the mobile application and check your order status directly, if available.
  • Check whether notifications are enabled in your phone settings.

Order is marked completed but has not been received.

What to do:

  • Check your order number.
  • Present your receipt or order confirmation to restaurant staff.
  • Ask them to verify the order before leaving.

Incorrect order status is displayed.

What to do:

  • Check your latest order confirmation.
  • Ask restaurant staff to verify the actual status of your order.

Unable to log in.

What to do:

  • Check your username and password.
  • Make sure your internet connection is stable.
  • Use the application's password recovery option if you cannot access your account.

Application will not open.

What to do:

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Close and reopen the application.
  • Check the official app store for available updates.

Cart suddenly becomes empty.

What to do:

  • Check your order history to determine whether the order was submitted.
  • If no order was placed, select your items again.
  • Review your cart before proceeding to checkout.

Application displays outdated menu information.

What to do:

  • Refresh the menu page.
  • Check whether the application has an available update.
  • Contact restaurant staff to confirm current food availability and prices.

Application keeps disconnecting.

What to do:

  • Switch to a more stable Wi-Fi or mobile data connection.
  • Reopen the application once the connection is restored.
  • Check your order history before attempting to submit another order.
